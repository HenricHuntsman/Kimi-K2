## 🔒 Legal & Ethical Guidelines

### Important Legal Information

All multimedia capabilities include strong legal and ethical guidelines to ensure responsible use.

#### Recording & Time-Shifting

**✅ ALLOWED (Personal Use):**
- Recording for personal time-shifting (watching later)
- Recording for archival on personal devices
- Recording for educational purposes (own use)
- Recording for accessibility (closed captions, etc.)
- Recording to preserve media you own
- Recording on a single device for personal viewing

**❌ NOT ALLOWED (Illegal):**
- Distributing recordings to others
- Sharing recordings online (uploading)
- Selling or profiting from recordings
- Public display of recordings
- Removing copy protection (DRM)
- Recording for commercial purposes
- Making copies for friends/family
- Monetizing recorded content

#### Copyright & Intellectual Property

**Respect Content Creators:**
- ✅ Support official platforms when possible
- ✅ Purchase music, shows, movies legally
- ✅ Subscribe to streaming services
- ✅ Buy merchandise to support creators
- ✅ Attend official events
- ✅ Follow creators on official channels
- ✅ Share official links (not recordings)

**Follow Local Laws:**
- ⚖️ Recording laws vary by country/region
- ⚖️ Check your local regulations
- ⚖️ Fair use varies by jurisdiction
- ⚖️ Personal use definitions differ
- ⚖️ Time-shifting may have limitations
- ⚖️ Seek legal advice if unsure

#### Best Practices

**Personal Use Guidelines:**
1. **Record only for yourself** - Don't share

3. **Delete after viewing** - Don't build massive libraries
4. **Use official services first** - Recording is last resort
5. **Support creators** - Buy their work when you can
6. **Respect copyright notices** - Don't ignore warnings
7. **Keep recordings private** - Never public
8. **Use appropriate tools** - Don't circumvent protection

**Ethical Considerations:**
- Content creators deserve compensation
- Recording impacts their revenue
- Support the entertainment industry
